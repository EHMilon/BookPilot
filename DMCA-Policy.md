BookPilot hosts original, transformative book summaries written by
contributors — key ideas retold in the author's own words — not
reproductions of book text. This policy explains how rights holders can
request removal of material they believe infringes their copyright, and
how we handle those requests.

## Filing a notice

Send a notice to **eh.milon.dev@gmail.com** with the subject
`DMCA Takedown — <book title or folder>`, or open an issue at
https://github.com/EHMilon/BookPilot. Include:

1. Your full name, organisation (if any), and contact details.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work (title, author, ISBN where
   known).
3. The exact paths in this repository you want removed (repo path or
   URL, e.g. `books/<slug>/summary.md`).
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not
   authorised by the rights holder, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is
   accurate and you are authorised to act on behalf of the rights
   holder.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may slow review. We may ask for clarification before
acting.

## Review and removal

Every valid notice is reviewed by a maintainer. Infringing material is
removed or disabled, normally within **14 days** of receiving a complete
notice. When content is removed, the corresponding manifest entry is
updated and the change is noted in the commit history.

Removal applies to the summary file(s) named in the notice. Manifest
metadata (title, author) that is purely factual may be retained unless
it is itself the subject of the complaint.

## Counter-notices

If your contribution was removed and you believe the removal was a
mistake, reply to the same thread with:

1. Your name and contact details.
2. The removed paths and why you believe the material is lawful
   (e.g. wholly original writing, not copied text).
3. Consent to the jurisdiction of the applicable courts for disputes
   arising from the material.

Restoration follows review of the counter-notice, typically within 14
days, unless the original complainant confirms ongoing proceedings.

## Repeat infringement

Accounts or contributors that repeatedly submit infringing material
after warnings may have their contributions removed and further
submissions refused.
