# Privacy Policy for BookPilot

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

## Overview

BookPilot ("we", "our", "the app") is a local-first reading companion: an
open library of original book summaries, an AI chat assistant, text-to-speech
audio, and personal notes. Most of your data never leaves your device. This
policy explains what stays on your device, what is transmitted off it, and
how to delete it.

## Data that stays on your device (never transmitted)

The following lives only in your phone's local storage and is never sent to
us or anyone else:

- Notes, summaries, and journal entries you create
- Chat history with the AI assistant
- Imported books and reading progress
- App settings, preferences, and streak data
- On-device search indexes

Uninstalling the app, or clearing app data via
Android Settings → Apps → BookPilot → Storage → Clear data, permanently
deletes all of it.

## Data transmitted off your device

| Data | Sent to | Why |
| ---- | ------- | --- |
| Device identifiers (push token, app-instance / device IDs) | Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), Adapty | Delivering notifications; managing subscriptions |
| Purchase history (Pro subscription status, plan, trial state) | Google Play, Adapty | Unlocking Pro features, restoring purchases |
| AI chat messages and book excerpts you ask about | Our secure API relay, then our AI providers | Generating the AI response you requested; not stored by us |
| Anonymous book/catalog requests (book IDs, search terms) | Our content host, Open Library, Google Books | Fetching summaries, metadata, and cover art |

We do not sell personal data to anyone. Data sent to the providers above is
used only to operate the app's features and is governed by their own privacy
policies (Google, Adapty, and our AI providers).

## Accounts

Account sign-in (email, Google, GitHub) is currently disabled in the app. We
do not collect names, email addresses, or passwords. If sign-in returns in a
future version, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data
from children under 13 beyond what is described above (device identifiers
required for push and subscriptions). Parents or guardians may request
deletion of a child's data at any time via the contact below.

## Data deletion (no account needed)

Because there is no account system, there is no login wall in front of
deletion:

1. **On-device data:** delete individual chats and notes inside the app, or
   wipe everything at once via Android Settings → Apps → BookPilot →
   Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
2. **Server-side traces (push token, subscription records):** email
   **eh.milon.dev@gmail.com** with the subject `Delete my data` from any
   address, and tell us your Play order ID (for purchase records) if
   relevant. We will confirm deletion within 30 days, including requesting
   removal from our processors (Adapty, Firebase) where they hold data
   linked to you.

This page itself is the deletion-request link referenced in our Play Store
data safety declaration.

## Data retention

- On-device data is kept until you delete it.
- AI requests are processed in memory to generate a response and are not
  stored by us; our providers may retain transient logs under their own
  policies.
- Purchase records are retained as long as required for subscription
  management, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations.

## Security

- All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.
- Local data benefits from Android's app-sandboxed storage.
- We collect the minimum data needed for the app to work and add nothing
  extra for advertising. The app shows no third-party ads and performs no
  ad tracking.

## Third-party links and services

The app may fetch content from external services (book metadata, covers, AI).
Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies when
following external links.

## Changes to this policy

with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes
